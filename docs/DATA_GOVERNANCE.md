# Data Governance

This document defines what data is open (public) versus closed (protected) within the Tri-CEA project.

## Open / Public Data (this repository)

This repository includes data collected from public sources only:

- County-level cost data: utilities, water, rent, fuel, taxes, labor, and wages
- Weather and sunlight exposure data
- Publicly available policy and regulatory documentation
- Workforce and labor market statistics from public sources

This data carries no personally identifiable information and is not subject to IRB restrictions.

## Closed / Protected Data (not in this repository)

The following data is not included here and is handled under restricted protocols:

- Raw survey data from producer, consumer, and community-organization surveys, collected under IRB oversight.
- Tribal community survey data, which follows data sovereignty principles: raw data is stored by or returned to the community's research office, kept password-protected on secured computers per IRB terms, and destroyed or returned to the community at project end. Only de-identified, aggregated results may be shared in future publications.
- Identifiable farmer survey data, handled under separate CITI-trained protocols.

## Why the Distinction Matters

Data sovereignty for tribal communities means the community, not the research team, retains control over how their data is stored, used, and shared. This repository respects that boundary by design, keeping all protected and identifiable data outside the public record.

## Questions About Data Provenance

If a variable appears to be missing, it may be:

1. Not yet compiled, as data collection is iterative and ongoing
2. Part of the closed and protected dataset described above
3. Available through the policy and regulation research track

For questions about specific datasets or their sources, contact the Tri-CEA Management Team through the project website (https://tricea.unm.edu).
